About "Extract with AI" & Data Privacy

The Extract with AI feature utilizes Google Gemini to accurately parse and extract your data. By enabling this feature, you agree to the following data processing terms:

• Account-Linked Processing: Enabling this feature will send the text/documents to Gemini for processing. This data is processed entirely through the Google account linked to the API key you provided. I do not store, access, or log your API key or data on my server.

• How Google Handles Your Data: Because the data stays inside the boundary of the account that owns the API key, Google’s data usage policy depends entirely on your specific API key's tier:

  • Paid Tier: Your data remains 100% private. Google is legally restricted from using your extracted data to train its models.
  • Free Tier: Google is allowed to check your data for human review and utilize it to train and improve its AI models.

If you are processing sensitive or confidential information, it is highly recommended to enable billing on your Google AI Studio account to guarantee total data privacy.
