How to Convert Statements

Extract, parse, and structure transactions from any PDF into Excel
Smart Extraction
  • Select Bank Statements
    Click "Select Statements" to choose one or more PDF bank statements (standard digital PDFs or scanned files).
  • Define Extraction Area (Interactive Tuning)
    Click "Define Extraction Area" to visually inspect pages and frame transactions. This ensures bank letterheads, customer address blocks, notices, and summary footers are excluded while capturing only table rows.
    ⚡ Auto Detect (Page): Analyzes layout and table borders to automatically snap a bounding box and column dividers around transactions on the active page.
    ✨ Auto Detect (All Pages): Automatically scans, detects, and adapts table boundaries across every page in the document in one go.
    🔄 Auto-apply to next pages: Check this box to automatically cascade manual adjustments on the current page forward to all subsequent pages.
    █ Draw Table / Resize: Drag a custom rectangle around the transaction area, or drag any of the 8 border handles to adjust coordinates.
    ║ Draw Columns: Click inside the table box to insert or reposition vertical dividers separating Date, Description, Cheque/Ref, Debit, Credit, and Balance.
    ═ Draw Rows: Place horizontal dividers to separate headers from data or isolate complex multi-line transactions.
    📋 Copy Prev Page: Instantly clones the previous page’s table area and column splits onto the current page (ideal for statements with consistent layouts).
    🚫 Skip Page: Marks non-transaction pages (cover pages, terms & conditions, marketing flyers) with a "PAGE SKIPPED" banner so they are omitted from extraction.
    🗑️ Clear Page / Clear All: Removes bounding boxes from the current page or resets all pages across the document.
    🔍 Zoom & Navigation: Use Zoom In/Out and Fit to Height for pixel-precise tuning, then click "Save Extraction Area" to confirm.
  • Set Password (If Protected)
    If statements are password-protected (e.g., PAN, Date of Birth, or Account Number), enter the password in the dialog and check "Apply password to all statements".
  • Choose Extraction Mode
    • Standard Digital Extraction: Ultra-fast native text parsing for normal selectable PDFs.
    • Enable Local OCR: Offline text recognition for scanned statements (requires local OCR module).
    • Extract with AI: Uses Google Gemini for high-accuracy parsing of complex multi-column or scanned statements.
  • Extract & Export to Excel
    Click "Extract & Parse Statements". The converter extracts transactions, aligns multiline descriptions, standardizes debit/credit amounts, and saves cleanly structured Excel sheets.
💡
Pro Tip: Click "Edit Columns" to customize output column headers (e.g., Date, Narration, Chq/Ref No, Withdrawal, Deposit, Balance) to match your accounting software.

Google Gemini API Key Setup

Required only for "Extract with AI" mode
Free & Private
  • Open Google AI Studio
    Visit aistudio.google.com/app/apikey and sign in with your Google account.
  • Generate Personal Key
    Click "Create API key" to generate your personal key. It is completely free for individual usage.
  • Save in Converter
    Paste your key into the Gemini API Key input field on the Bank Statement Converter screen or in Vertex Settings.

Security & Data Privacy

Your financial documents remain 100% private
Zero Telemetry

🛡️ Local Parsing

Standard text parsing and Local OCR run completely offline on your computer. Zero financial data is transmitted over the internet.

🔑 Volatile Passwords

Statement passwords are kept only in volatile memory during the active session and are never written to disk or logs.

📁 Direct Output

Generated Excel workbooks are saved directly to your chosen folder.

🔒 Note on Data & AI Processing
Vertex does not collect, track, or store any of your financial data or statement records on any server. All AI-powered processing connects directly from your machine to Google Gemini exclusively using your own personal API key—the developer of Vertex has no access to your credentials, bank statements, or extracted data. Furthermore, AI extraction is strictly opt-in and is executed only when you explicitly select the "Extract with AI" option.
